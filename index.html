Work

Detecting Human–AI Collaborative Academic Writing
MSc Dissertation, 2026

Most AI-text detectors treat authorship as binary: human or AI. That framing breaks down for the way students actually use ChatGPT — drafting, editing, polishing. I built a four-class classifier that separates pure human and pure AI writing from two collaborative in-between cases, and used SHAP to turn the model's output into plain-English explanations an educator could actually act on.

0.78 macro-recall (XGBoost + BERT)
13 handcrafted linguistic features
5,472 balanced training samples

About
