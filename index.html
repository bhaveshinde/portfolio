Four-Class Human–AI Collaborative Writing Detector
MSc Dissertation · 2026

Most AI-text detectors treat authorship as binary — human or AI. That framing breaks down for how students actually use ChatGPT: drafting, editing, polishing. I built a four-class classifier that separates pure human and pure AI writing from two collaborative in-between cases, and used SHAP to turn the model's output into plain-English explanations an educator could act on.

  • 0.78 macro-recall with XGBoost on BERT embeddings, a 62% relative improvement over a published RoBERTa baseline (0.48)
  • Engineered 13 handcrafted linguistic features across lexical, syntactic, sentiment, and structural dimensions
  • Validated results with bootstrap confidence intervals and an internal binary baseline, not a single headline number
